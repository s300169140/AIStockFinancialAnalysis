AI Stock Analysis (Android app, package com.aistock.analysis)

1. Who we are

"AI Stock Analysis" (the "App") is operated by Lennon Li ("we", "us", "our"). Contact: lennonli999@gmail.com.

2. Summary

We collect the minimum data needed to run the App: a generated device ID (so we can count your three free analyses), and — only if you choose to subscribe to PRO — your email, name, and profile picture via Google Sign-In, plus the purchase token from Google Play. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

CategoryDataWhy we collect itHow long we keep it

  • Your real name, address, or phone number (unless you choose to email us).
  • Location (GPS, network, or coarse).
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone, or camera content.
  • Advertising identifiers (Android AAID).
  • Biometric data.
  • Health, financial account balances, or banking credentials.
  • Browser history or activity on other apps.

5. How we use third parties

5.1 Google Sign-In

When you sign in to subscribe to PRO, your Google account info is shared with us under Google's terms. We request only the openid, email, and profile scopes. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5.2 Google Play Billing

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment information directly; we never see your credit card or bank details. We only receive a purchase token, the order ID, and renewal/expiry timestamps.

5.3 DeepSeek (AI analysis provider)

When you request an analysis, we send the ticker symbol and a fixed prompt template to DeepSeek's API to generate the report. We do not send your email, name, device ID, or any other identifying information to DeepSeek. The request is associated only with our server-level API key.

5.4 Hosting

Our backend runs on standard cloud infrastructure. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We do not transfer your data to data brokers or marketers.

6. Children

The App is intended for users 18 years and older. Investment-related content is not appropriate for minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a minor has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

7. Security

We use HTTPS/TLS for all client-server communication, hash and salt session secrets, keep our database access-restricted, and apply standard security patches. No system is 100% secure, but we treat your data with care.

8. Your rights and how to delete your data

You can delete your account and all associated data by emailing us at lennonli999@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my account" from the Google account you signed in with. We will:

  • Confirm your identity (matching email).
  • Delete your account row, your subscription record, and all linked device data within 14 days.
  • Retain purchase records as required by tax law (anonymized).

EU/UK residents under GDPR, California residents under CCPA, and other jurisdictions may have additional rights (access, portability, correction, objection). Use the same email above to exercise them; we'll respond within the timeframe required by your local law.

9. International transfers

Our servers may be located in the United States. By using the App, you understand that your data may be processed in countries other than your own. We rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards for cross-border transfers where required.

10. Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy, we'll bump the "Last updated" date above. For significant changes (e.g., new data categories), we'll notify you in-app before the change takes effect.
